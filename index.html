Thunderstrike JS

A reimplementation of the 1990 DOS game Thunderstrike by Millennium. It contains no game data: to play, supply your own copy of the original DOS release (the folder containing TS.EXE, or a .zip of it).

Drop the game .zip or folder here, or click to choose a .zip

Files are read locally in your browser and remembered on this device only.

Default keys: Z/X turn, L/, climb and dive, P thrust (tap twice for turbo), Space fire, F1 options, F2 play, F3 craft, F4 stats, F10 pause, Esc menu.
